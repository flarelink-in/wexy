How it works

Three steps.
No skills needed.

  1. 01

    Describe it

    Type an idea, or pick one. Meta's models plan the scene and write how it moves and how it looks, and your browser checks that code on your device before it runs.

    “A sakura shrine at twilight, with spirit dragons”
    1. Planning the scene
    2. Building the world
    3. Testing it on your device
  2. 02

    Play with it

    It's alive, not a recording. Drag to look around. Slide to change the spin, the wind or the glow. Save a few camera views and the camera glides between them.

    Disk spin
    Jet power
    Glow
  3. 03

    Share it

    Pick wide, vertical or square. Snap a photo, or record a video in HD or Full HD. Your computer draws every frame at full quality.

    Wide Vertical Square
    Reels · Shorts · TikTok · YouTube1080p

Made to share

Vertical, wide or square

Framed for Reels, Shorts and TikTok as well as YouTube. The frame guide shows exactly what will be in the shot before you press Record.

Made on your device

No per-video fee. Your graphics card makes the video; your instructions are sent to Meta's models.

Every frame at full quality

Every frame is drawn at full quality, even on a modest laptop. A slower computer just takes a little longer to finish.

Built with Meta AI

Meta's AI does the hard parts.

Muse Spark

The director

Turns your sentence into a plan: what's in the scene, how it moves, how it's lit and where the camera goes.

Muse Spark

The builder

Writes the code that brings the plan to life on your graphics card, and fixes it if a check fails.

In this preview, the studio's worlds are playground examples written by hand; live creation with Meta's models is being connected. The 3D worlds, the controls and the video export are real, and they run on your computer.

For developers: Wexy runs on NullGraph, an open-source WebGPU engine.
